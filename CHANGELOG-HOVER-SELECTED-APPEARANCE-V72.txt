Electrical Simu CKT v72 — Hover and selected appearance

- Hovering over an unselected workspace component leaves its original artwork colour unchanged.
- No selection border or filled background appears on hover.
- Clicking/selecting a component colours the actual SVG symbol blue and shows a thin, close-fitting border.
- Component reference tags and comments remain independent of the symbol tint.
- Existing workspace interactions and library files are preserved.
